Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 22, 2026 • Last Updated: September 22, 2026

At PawRx: Pet Meds & Care ("PawRx", "we", "our", or "us"), we prioritize the privacy and security of you and your pets. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding the collection, storage, use, and protection of information when you access or use our mobile application and related services.

We strictly comply with Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines (including Guideline 5.1.1 on Data Collection and Storage), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable privacy laws.

🚫

No Third-Party Ads

We never sell your data or serve invasive third-party ad trackers.

🛡️

End-to-End Safety

Caregiver sync is encrypted in transit and securely partitioned.

🗑️

Total User Control

You can export or permanently delete your account data at any time.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information to provide pet medication tracking, multi-caregiver coordination, and emergency safety features:

  • Account Information: Your email address and hashed password to authenticate your account and securely sync across your devices.
  • Pet Profile Details: Pet name, species, breed, age, weight, allergies, and veterinary clinic contact numbers.
  • Medication & Health Records: Medication names, dosages, schedules, dietary pairing requirements, administration history, and optional recovery photos for healing journals.
  • Household & Caregiver Attribution: Caregiver display names and shift handover notes used exclusively to prevent double-dosing across family members and pet sitters.
  • StoreKit Subscription Status: Receipt verification tokens processed directly through Apple's App Store / StoreKit 2 APIs to manage your 3-day trial and subscription access. We do NOT collect or store your payment card numbers.

2. Device Permissions (iOS)

PawRx may request optional device permissions solely to support core in-app features:

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used exclusively when you choose to photograph your pet, update a pet avatar, or log a healing journal progression picture. Photos are stored securely and never analyzed for advertising.
  • Local Notifications: Used to deliver timely dosing alerts and upcoming vaccination or appointment reminders directly to your device.

3. How We Protect & Share Your Data

We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your personal or pet health information. Data is shared only under the following explicit circumstances:

  • With Your Household Caregivers: Other caregivers whom you explicitly invite using your unique Household Share Code can view medication schedules and logs to coordinate dosing.
  • Public Lost Pet Collar QR Tags: If you generate a scannable collar tag, only the specific emergency contact phone and critical medical alerts you designate will be viewable by anyone who scans the tag.
  • Service Providers: Encrypted cloud hosting and Apple StoreKit payment verification systems necessary to operate the service.

4. Data Retention, Export & Deletion Rights

You retain total autonomy over your data:

  • Data Export: You can download a complete PDF medical dossier or JSON export of your pet’s records directly from the app dashboard at any time.
  • Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account, pet profiles, and all historical dosing logs by going to Profile Settings > Delete Account, or by emailing privacy@pawrx.app. Deletions are processed within 24 hours.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

PawRx does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will delete that information immediately.
