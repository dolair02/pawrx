📚 Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 3-Day Free Trial work?

When you start your trial, you get 100% full access to all PawRx All-Star features (unlimited pets, multi-caregiver sync, double-dose safety lockouts, and emergency QR tags) for 3 full days without charge. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, your chosen subscription ($2.99/month or $29.99/year) will automatically begin via Apple StoreKit.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

All PawRx subscriptions are securely managed through your Apple ID:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your Name / Apple ID at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select PawRx and tap Cancel Subscription or modify your plan.

How does Double-Dose Protection prevent accidental overdoses?

Whenever any family member or pet sitter marks a medication as given, PawRx records an immutable timestamp and caregiver attribution. If another person tries to administer that same dose, PawRx displays a high-priority warning modal detailing who gave it, the exact time given, and a countdown timer until the next safe dose interval.

How do I invite family members or temporary pet sitters?

Open the app and navigate to the 👥 Household tab. Tap "Copy Household Share Code" or generate an instant QR code. Your family members or pet sitters can enter this code in their app to instantly join your household with zero complex setup.

How do I contact emergency veterinary services?

Tap the 🚨 SOS button in the top navigation bar at any time. You get instant 1-tap dialing to the 24/7 ASPCA Animal Poison Control hotline (888-426-4435), Pet Poison Helpline, and turn-by-turn directions to your nearest emergency veterinary clinic.
